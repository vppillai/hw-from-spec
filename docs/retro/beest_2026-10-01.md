### references/agent-ops.md
- 2026-09-30 [mechanical] A 120° twisted D-journal is chiral: no proper rotation turns a +120° twist going +y into a +120° twist going −y. Either two mirrored journal types or let the mirrored side run its phases in the other order (0 / 240 / 120): the gait is identical *(evidence: CC-004.)*

### references/case-pipeline.md
- 2026-09-30 [tooling] OpenSCAD 2026.09.30 snapshot with `--backend=Manifold` exports the Wankel housing in 0.07 s where 2021.01 (CGAL) took minutes; the Homebrew cask `openscad@snapshot` refuses to coexist with `openscad`, the dmg installs beside it as a second app — ENV.md, CC-001.
- 2026-09-30 [mechanical] A 360° sweep of every body PAIR per level, including pins, pegs and caps as discs, finds what a bar-only sweep misses: J4 and J5 pass 10.6 mm apart (Ø11 bosses touched → Ø10), the hip peg grazes bar k by 5 mm² (→ a 3 mm dog-leg). Both are invisible in any single pose render *(evidence: CC-003, CC-006.)*

### references/dfm-printed-enclosure.md
- 2026-09-30 [dfm] The 0.9.1 print-DFM gate wants one process row per print target; colour bodies (strokes 1.0, skins 0.6) need their own target (`home_fdm_colour`, row `home_fdm_04_colour_body`) with their own census bars *(evidence: CC-007.)*
- 2026-10-01 [dfm] OpenSCAD `text(size=)` is not the cap height (DIN Alternate: cap = 0.72 × size). A rule that says "cap ≥ 4 mm" needs the H measured once per font and the size derived — gen/beest.py cap_to_size, CC-011.
- 2026-10-01 [dfm] At a 4 mm cap no common bold font keeps stroke ≥ 1.0 AND counters ≥ 0.45 after padding except a condensed DIN; measure (erode / inscribe) before picking a legend font *(evidence: CC-011.)*
- 2026-10-01 [tooling] A standing D-shaft built as D-extrude + cylinder + ramp unions fragmented into 13 shells; ONE cylinder minus bevelled flat cuts is manifold by construction — gen/beest.py d_shaft.
- 2026-10-01 [dfm] Bambu Studio's arranger nests concave outlines (Jansen triangles with windows) and aborts with "gcode path conflicts"; pre-place such plates (`--arrange 0`) but leave multi-colour plates to the arranger, which also places the wipe tower *(evidence: CC-010.)*

### references/pitfalls.md
- 2026-09-30 [mechanical] A planar Jansen leg needs three Y-levels: the conflict graph of its seven bodies over a full cycle has a triangle (c–k–T1); a 2-level layout cannot exist. Compute the graph from a bar sweep before drawing a single link *(evidence: CC-002.)*
- 2026-09-30 [mechanical] A crank web polygon drawn in its LOCAL frame (hub at the origin) placed with a rigid transform computed from MACHINE-frame joints lands 29 mm off; 158 mm² of false overlap. One frame per polygon family, asserted — gen/beest.py level_bodies.
- 2026-09-30 [mechanical] A ratchet wheel or pawl mounted flipped reverses its direction; identical ratchet parts on both ends of a shaft must be mounted with the SAME face outward on both ends, not mirrored — gen/beest.py assembly_items.
- 2026-09-30 [mechanical] A spool-and-loop rubber band stretches 3× at most and a loop long enough for five spool turns does not fit a 130 mm machine; a TWISTED band (the aeroplane motor) stores ~100 twists of near-constant torque in 55 mm *(evidence: CC-005.)*
- 2026-09-30 [agents] A brake lever's bistability is a centroid question: engaged = centroid right of the pivot (tooth down by gravity), released = centroid left (rests on the rail). Two CHECKS rows settle what a render cannot — gen/beest.py.
- 2026-10-01 [dfm] Glyph holes from an SVG path: classify rings by COVERAGE (bigger ring covers the smaller), never by a representative point — a D's outer ring tested "inside" its own counter and the letter vanished; a one-body-per-character CHECK row catches it *(evidence: CC-011.)*
