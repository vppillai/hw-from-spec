## 5. Reference patch stubs (bullets to append; generalise the numbers, label the worked example, keep the evidence pointer at the end)

### references/agent-ops.md
- 2026-09-30 [agents] Eight parallel OpenSCAD CGAL exports plus parallel censuses plus six offscreen PNG renders exhausted the process table (fork: EAGAIN, load average above 70) and the host restarted a second time: cap the geometry pipeline at ~3 concurrent CAD jobs, run mesh checks and renders sequentially, launch long chains in the background with an EXIT line and poll with a background `until grep` *(evidence: CC-009.)*

### references/dfm-printed-enclosure.md
- 2026-09-30 [mechanical] Nearest-point projection onto a peritrochoid to anchor arc text landed 15 mm off-centre: the trochoid's waist at the minor axis is a local MAXIMUM of the radius (slightly concave for K ≈ 6.4), so the point nearest to a far anchor lies on a lobe. Anchor by polar angle (the curve point whose polar angle equals the target), never by nearest point *(evidence: CC-006.)*
- 2026-09-30 [mechanical] An eccentric shaft prints support-free only when journal radius + eccentricity ≤ eccentric radius (the journals then sit inside the eccentric's footprint) and the shaft is split so the eccentric disc is a bed face; write that inequality as a check row *(evidence: CC-004.)*

### references/pitfalls.md
- 2026-09-30 [tooling] The thin-wall census with trimesh's rtree ray engine needs memory proportional to rays × candidate triangles: a 15 000 mm² plate at 4 samples/mm² took 4.7 GB and at the target density of 10 /mm² four parallel bodies exceeded a 24 GB machine's RAM (the host restarted). With `embreex` installed trimesh uses Embree and the same body censuses in 2.7 s at 755 MB; pin `embreex` next to trimesh in the install line and run the census one body at a time *(evidence: CC-009.)*
- 2026-09-30 [dfm] A raised legend fails the void gate in every font: the acute crotches of A / K / N / W and the counters of e / 4 / R narrow below 1.0 at any cap that fits an 11 mm band (swept 11 fonts × 3 caps × 3 pads). Meet the rule by geometry, not by font: morphologically CLOSE the glyph polygons at the void gate (buffer +g/2 then −g/2, after placement so inter-letter gaps count too) and gate the inter-letter gap separately *(evidence: CC-006, out/wankel/home_fdm/CHECKS.md rows 16–19.)*
- 2026-09-30 [dfm] Any radial hole exiting a CURVED face leaves a feather-edge wedge (material between the hole wall and the surface tapers to 0.03 mm at the sides of the exit) that the census flags as a free-standing wedge; a shallow spot face only moves the feather to its own rim. Give every such hole a fused BLOCK with a flat outer face perpendicular to the hole axis (port flanges, plug boss) — housing census rounds 1–3, CC-008.
- 2026-09-30 [mechanical] Internal-gear meshing with a small tooth difference (14:21, difference 7) is tip-limited: with standard addendum 0.8 m and 0.25 backlash the sweep over 216 shaft angles gave 0.062 mm running clearance although the interference area was 0; stub teeth (0.7 m) plus 0.4 backlash gave 0.161. Gate the minimum running clearance of a gear sweep as its own row (≥ 0.15 for printed gears), not only the interference area *(evidence: CC-002.)*
- 2026-09-30 [dfm] A pocket cut part-height into a vertical flank leaves a pocket ceiling (an overhang) however shallow; make flank recesses full-height dishes in the 2D profile so every rotor wall stays vertical *(evidence: CC-007.)*

### references/print-dfm.md
- 2026-09-30 [dfm] A 96-gon lobe unioned with an offset curve leaves 0.03 mm hairline notches at the junctions; the area-weighted census never samples them, the print-DFM point-contact rule (section planes) does. Close the outline union with a MITRE buffer (+1 / −1, join_style mitre) before any body uses it — print_dfm round 1 housing, CC-012.

### references/release-and-cut.md
- 2026-09-30 [process] A design-only showcase still needs the owner's print-order and knob-reading story: the fit coupon plate with matching pegs prints first (plate 1) and the yaml knobs are read off it before plates 2 and 3 *(evidence: CC-005, README.)*
