# Blind review B — hw-from-spec 0.4.1 — manufacturing / DFM engineer lens (2026-09-28)

Reviewer: independent, blind (repo the skill repo at 0.4.1 only; no project material, no chat history, no other reviewer's output).
Lens: PCB fab/assembly at a contract fab, MJF PA12 and SLA at a print service, home FDM (Bambu, 0.4 nozzle, PLA/PETG), CNC aluminium.
Question asked: would this skill get a team to a manufacturable enclosure + board in ONE DFM round, and are its rules technically sound?

## 2. Verdict

The skill would get a competent team to a **vendor-clean MJF part at JLC3DP in one DFM round** — the census-as-FAIL-gate, the no-waiver rule, the canonical-STL identity, the API-verdict procedure, the probe method, the coupon-first FDM preset and the mirrored two-target yaml are stronger than most professional mechanical teams' practice, and the failure history is honestly recorded. It would **not** yet reliably get a team to a *manufacturable* design in the wider sense, for three reasons: several rules calibrated on one vendor's colour checker are stated as material physics (living hinges, engraved text, fillets, the 1.2 wall, the 2.0 rim), so a team at another vendor or on SLA/CNC will over- or under-design; the automated census has known blind spots (wedges listed not gated, normal-direction rays only, untested recall) and the only control behind them is a human looking at a heat map; and the classic DFM envelope — tolerance stack, insert/boss engineering, material ratings, post-processing effects, fastener/adhesive compatibility with PA12 — is absent, while a "process tolerance ±0.1" claim in the gate template is wrong in the direction that produces the next crack. SLA and CNC coverage is two sentences each and does not justify their place in the skill's description.

## 3. The five changes that would most reduce DFM iterations

1. **Parameterise the print targets and relabel the physics** (B-33, B-02, B-03, B-04, B-15, B-35): a `print_targets:` block in `project.yaml` (vendor, process, wall/void/red lines, design margin, published tolerance, checker URL + date) read by `thin_wall_census.py`; rewrite §1 so each rule says whether it is the vendor's checker, the owner's bar, or the material — and restore tangent fillets, snap features in PA12 and engraved text ≥ the void line as allowed options.
2. **Close the census blind spots and test recall** (B-12, B-13, B-14): gate wedges by the width of the sub-gate band, add nearest-opposing-face-in-any-direction as a gated metric (the class that produced the length-dependent flag), scale samples by surface area, and add recall primitives (0.8 mm rib, 0.6 mm slit) to the selftest so "0 FAIL" means something.
3. **Replace the ±0.1 waiver with measured tolerance and a worst-case stack** (B-01, B-37, B-21a): first-article caliper table on every gated wall and bore of every received part feeds a `tolerance` per target; the interference/clearance check adds a worst-case row per mating pair; the "PASS by design" row becomes PASS on the measured spread or goes to INFO.
